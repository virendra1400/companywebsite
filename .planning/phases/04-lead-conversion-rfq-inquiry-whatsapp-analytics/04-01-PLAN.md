<objective>
Deliver the independent, no-server-dependency vertical slice of Phase 4: a persistent WhatsApp click-to-chat CTA in the site chrome (LEAD-06/D-05) that fires a named `whatsapp_click` analytics event (LEAD-07/D-06), plus the provider-agnostic `trackEvent()` wrapper (ANALY-01) that all Phase 4 conversion events route through. This plan also establishes the Vitest `unit` test project every later Phase 4 plan depends on.

After this plan a real user can tap a WhatsApp CTA from the header (desktop) or the mobile nav sheet (anywhere on the site) and open a pre-filled `wa.me` chat, and that tap fires a distinct named conversion event through a wrapper that works regardless of which analytics vendor is eventually wired in.

Purpose: Satisfy LEAD-06 site-wide WhatsApp reach and the event-firing half of LEAD-07/ANALY-01 with zero backend coupling, and unblock the unit-test harness for the form plans.
Output: `src/lib/analytics.ts`, shared `WhatsAppIcon`, `WhatsAppCta` client component, header/mobile wiring, standardized copy, Vitest `unit` project, unit + e2e tests.
</objective>
