<objective>
Layer per-item stagger on top of the section-level reveal for the grid-shaped surfaces, and land the one deliberate directional-slide surface this phase allows.

Purpose: section-level reveal alone makes a 4-up card grid arrive as a single slab. A tight 50ms stagger is what reads as considered rather than mechanical, and the cap is what keeps that true when the catalog grows.

Output: six wiring edits and an RTL directional-mirroring assertion added to the existing Arabic spec — the third Wave 0 gap named in 09-VALIDATION.md.

## Resolved discretion (CONTEXT.md left these open; decided here, with reasons)

- **Stagger increment:** `STAGGER_MS = 50`, `STAGGER_CAP = 8` (already fixed in 09-01). Tight end of the 40-120ms range, per CONTEXT.md's own lean and 09-UI-SPEC section 3.
- **Product/Insights grids get stagger, not a whole-grid fade.** These are the two grids most likely to grow; the cap is precisely what makes staggering safe at scale, so there is no reason to treat them differently from a 4-item FeatureGrid.
- **StatsBand figures stay static — no count-up.** Per CONTEXT.md's stated default. A count-up would require converting the figure into a client leaf plus a `requestAnimationFrame` counter, which D-01's restrained register does not ask for and which adds reduced-motion and layout-stability edge cases for no trust gain.
- **TrustBarBlock and CertStripBlock get section-level reveal only, no per-item stagger.** Not a scope cut — D-07's uniform reveal is delivered at block level by plan 09-02 and covers both. Item-level stagger is explicitly planner's discretion in CONTEXT.md, and both surfaces have a concrete structural reason to skip it: TrustBar is a centred `flex flex-wrap` logo row where a stretched wrapper fights `items-center`, and CertStrip's halal `CertCard` carries `col-span-2`, which a wrapper div would silently swallow (the wrapper, not the card, becomes the grid child). Wrapping either would trade a subtle motion gain for a real layout regression.
- **Directional slide (D-10) is used exactly once: ExportProcessBlock steps, `direction="start"`.** A numbered inquiry-to-delivery sequence is the one surface where "arrives along the reading direction" carries meaning rather than decoration, which is the bar D-10 sets ("use sparingly and only where direction genuinely reads as intentional").
</objective>
