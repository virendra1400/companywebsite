# CONTENT_PLAYBOOK — VNP Global

All copy on the site follows this document. Honesty rules in PROJECT_MEMORY §7 override everything here.

## 1. Voice & Tone

- **Voice:** a competent export manager who knows food processing — precise, calm, direct, quietly proud of the process. Not a marketer.
- Sentence style: short. Concrete nouns and numbers. Active voice. One idea per sentence.
- **Specifics beat adjectives.** Wrong: "world-class premium quality mango pulp". Right: "Aseptically processed Alphonso and Totapuri mango pulp, packed in 215 kg bag-in-drum."
- Banned words: world-class, best-in-class, leading, premium (as bare adjective), cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, unparalleled, one-stop, synergy. Banned patterns: fake urgency, exclamation marks, rhetorical questions in headings.
- Allowed confidence: "export-grade", "documented", "audit-ready", "consistent" — because each is demonstrable.
- International readability: plain English, no idioms; many readers are ESL (Gulf/SEA buyers). Grade-8 reading level for marketing copy; technical precision in spec sections.

## 2. Messaging Hierarchy (the one story, told everywhere)

1. **What:** IQF frozen vegetables, aseptic fruit pulps, and value-added products from India, built for export.
2. **Proof:** manufactured at an operating export-oriented facility (Tasawade MIDC, Karad); in-house cold storage; QC and batch documentation; certifications registered/in progress (named, with status).
3. **How we de-risk you (the new-supplier objection, answered head-on):** open specs, sample program, published terms, third-party inspection welcome, virtual plant tour, 24-hour response SLA.
4. **Who for:** importers & distributors (Gulf first) and food processors (pulp for beverage/dairy/food manufacturing).

Every page answers, in order: *what can I buy → why should I trust you → what do I do next.*

## 3. Headline & CTA Formulas

- Hero H1 formula: `[product category truth] + [export/documentation promise]`. Approved options (pick in implementation, don't invent new ones without logging in DECISION_LOG):
  - "Export-grade frozen vegetables and fruit pulps from India."
  - "IQF vegetables and aseptic fruit pulps, documented for export."
- Hero subhead: "From sample carton to full container — processed at our group facility in Karad, Maharashtra, with batch-level quality documentation." *(adjust to verified facts)*
- Section H2s: statement-of-fact style ("Processed and packed for export", "Certifications, in the open", "How we work with new buyers"). Never question-style ("Why choose us?").
- CTA copy (exact strings): Primary **"Request a Quote"** · sample **"Request a Sample"** · secondary **"Download Spec Sheet (PDF)"** · tertiary **"View specifications"** · WhatsApp **"Chat on WhatsApp"** · tour **"Book a Virtual Plant Tour"**. No "Learn more", no "Read more", no "Submit".
